# Historical notes: Latin Christian writing around 1400

“Style of 1400” is not one uniform style. Texts varied by region, institution, genre, author, and whether they were devotional, scholastic, liturgical, or vernacular.

This prototype uses broad, generic Christian devotional language. It does **not** claim to reproduce a specific manuscript, author, local rite, or exact late-medieval usage.

For a historically grounded release:
1. Identify the intended region and genre.
2. Build a source catalogue from reliable critical editions and manuscript catalogues.
3. Keep quotations separate from generated compositions.
4. Store bibliographic details and page/folio references for every source excerpt.
5. Have Latin and medieval-history specialists review templates.
6. Never assume a modern prayer is historically attested merely because it is written in Latin.

Liturgical texts and sacramental formulas require particular care. This tool is for drafts and study, not authorization to perform liturgical rites.
